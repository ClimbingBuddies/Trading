# Trading product acceptance specification

**Version:** 1.0 — 26 October 2026  
**Applies to:** [Reliable Research and Decision Lab](../trading-reliability-and-decision-plan.md)  
**Review:** [Formal product review](../project-audits/PRODUCT-REVIEW-2026-10-26.md)

These scenarios specify future acceptance; they are **not recorded passing results**. Attach actual commands, source/deployment/API versions, sanitised outputs and limitations to the work item when executed. Use isolated fixtures for fault injection, cross-user cases and synthetic returns. Do not seed invented shared production calls or modify historical outcomes for testing.

## User journeys

| Journey | User outcome | Required behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| J1 — Morning review | Know what changed and what needs attention. | Show due market date, fresh/old/missing research, last successful completion and next expected update; one actionable explanation per blocker. A monitor check does not imply the research ran. |
| J2 — Understand a call | Explain why an AI action exists. | Source/evidence dates, model/method version, thesis, risks, intended horizon, comparator and original/update history are inspectable. Distinguish sourced facts, opinion and inference. |
| J3 — Follow an instrument | Know whether a watched asset can actually be scored. | Saving a watcher does not duplicate shared AI work. Show configuration/readiness and missing requirements; unsupported assets remain explicitly unsupported or research-only. |
| J4 — Assess outcomes | Understand what happened without confusing it with personal investment return. | Explain entry rule, selected horizon, allocation/cash/cost assumptions, matured/pending/blocked/excluded counts and price-only/total-return status. Preserve losses and old model versions. |
| J5 — Operate/recover | Restore an interrupted stage safely. | Correlate controller, research, publication and evaluator receipts; resume the logical run without duplicate calls, quota loops or historical rewrites. |

## Acceptance scenarios

| ID | Scenario | Pass criterion | Evidence owner / phase |
|---|---|---|---|
| AT-01 | Production/source/API reconciliation | Actual deployment/artifact identity, branch/source, database/API contracts and active/retired interfaces are recorded. The same source reproduces the shared page and read contracts. No reset, blind old migration or replacement of unrecovered production work. | Engineering; slice A |
| AT-02 | Reproducible build and preview | A clean frozen-lock install, build and relevant tests succeed with pinned tools/dependencies; no disabled TLS/signature/checksum checks. Preview uses controlled test fixtures/data and cannot send production notifications or start production analytical jobs. Record promotion and compatible rollback checks. | Engineering/release; slice A |
| AT-03 | Freshness and lineage | Old AI, missing Technical inputs, stale Convergence, weekend/holiday and failed Opportunity fixtures produce explicit states. Current aggregates use only eligible compatible sources; history remains accessible. “Today” is true in the displayed timezone. | Engineering + research; slice B |
| AT-04 | Fresh pilot publication | Three supported shares and one validated unleveraged ETF form the declared pilot. Each fresh eligible item saves one prospective call or a documented failure; expected failures do not count as usable coverage. A controlled real eligible item verifies the actual backend-to-browser path. | Backend/research; slice B |
| AT-05 | Retry, concurrency and partial failure | Repeat publication, simultaneous workers, timeout after commit, quota denial and one bad instrument do not create duplicates or prevent unrelated eligible work. Persist truthful stage states; retry only after a meaningful recovery condition. | Backend; slice B |
| AT-06 | Reliability accounting | Expected-stage denominator and due-calendar revision are fixed before the window. All statuses are visible; failed/blocked due stages count against availability, not as success. Exclude only genuinely not-due events under the recorded calendar. Evaluations distinguish immature pending from overdue processing. | Operations; slice B |
| AT-07 | Independent numerical reproduction | Recalculate eligible entry/mark/exit dates, exposure, costs, cash, underlying/benchmark returns and drawdown from pinned observations. Use deterministic tolerances defined before comparison; fixtures include loss, zero, missing return and overlapping reviews. A trial mean is not labelled a portfolio return. | Calculation reviewer; slice B |
| AT-08 | Integrity, corrections and status | Original calls, evidence and outcomes resist update/delete; repeat writes are idempotent. Late/corrected prices and method/calendar changes follow explicit append/withhold rules. NOT_ENTERED is terminal as defined; a same-date data gap cannot display a misleading verified result. Never backfill a call's earlier knowledge. | Backend + UI; slice B |
| AT-09 | ETF methodology | For each ETF, provider identity, split/distribution semantics, calendar, costs and comparator are verified. An instrument cannot claim excess return against itself. Price-only results say price-only; a total-return claim requires a verified cash/distribution policy without double adjustment. | Research + calculations; slice C/D |
| AT-10 | FX methodology | Define BUY/directional semantics and funded exposure without implying leveraged orders. Reproduce both pair directions, quote/base conversion, spread/slippage and carry assumptions using aligned observations. No QQQ-equity model substitution. Missing cost/funding evidence withholds net claims. | Research + calculations; slice D |
| AT-11 | Shared/private access matrix | Two distinct permanent users and a signed-out session exercise intended shared reads, private watchlists/notes/holdings and privileged write denial. Changing owner IDs or calling publication/evaluation functions cannot expose another user's context or obtain write authority. No credentials appear in frontend builds, logs or evidence. | Security reviewer; before promotion |
| AT-12 | Migration/recovery | Record current schema/API versions and backup/restore prerequisites. Demonstrate isolated restoration/compatible rollback or fix-forward against representative fixtures. A failed migration, stale worker or lost response does not erase original decisions or require disabling RLS. Do not use production-private dumps as casual test fixtures. | Release/operations; before promotion |
| AT-13 | Evidence and AI-output validation | Enforce typed action/horizon/method fields, valid instrument identity, cutoff/source references and claim-level evidence. Exercise fabricated URLs, stale/duplicate claims, malformed output, unsupported certainty and instructions embedded in retrieved material. Reject or hold invalid output; no silent Buy fallback. Model commentary is not a trusted executable instruction. | Research/engineering; slice B |
| AT-14 | Cost/capacity and change control | Record a real initial usage baseline and approved provider/model limits. Under representative concurrency, quotas and timeouts produce bounded queues and explicit status. Shared research is deduplicated; unchanged evidence is reused only under freshness/licensing rules. Store model/prompt/method version and measured usage; changes do not merge performance cohorts silently. | Operations/engineering; before universe expansion |
| AT-15 | Desktop/mobile/keyboard journeys | Execute J1–J4 at desktop and 390 × 844. Controls and selected instrument/list remain readable; tables scroll inside their container without document overflow. Focus, drawer closure, signed-out, loading, empty, error, retry and scope/horizon transitions work. Preserve bookmarks through navigation changes. | Product/UI reviewer; each affected release |
| AT-16 | Release readiness and pause controls | No unresolved P0 affecting the release slice. Engineering records exact source/schema/tests; operations confirms due-stage state and recovery; research confirms method/comparator. Verify a bounded generation pause that leaves historical reads/evaluation intact where safe. No automatic live trading, notification send or provider-spend expansion. Roles may be performed by available collaborators; do not invent separate agents or approvals as substitutes for evidence. | Release owner; every promotion |
| AT-17 | Product usefulness | Walk through five practical tasks: identify stale research, explain a call, find a blocker, compare an outcome, follow an asset. Record completion/misinterpretation and user feedback in a bounded review. Correct critical misunderstandings before adding more dashboard widgets. No covert user tracking or new analytics service is required. | Product/owner; slices B/C |
| AT-18 | Predictive evaluation | Predeclare eligible universe, model/method, action policy, horizons, comparator, costs and evaluation window. Keep holdout/forward evidence separate from tuning; account for correlation, selection, survivorship, missingness and regime coverage. Report uncertainty and adverse periods. A no-edge or inconclusive conclusion passes honest evaluation; a profitable-AI claim needs evidence beyond operational tests. | Research reviewer; later learning reviews |

## Definition of a release evidence packet

Record task IDs and findings addressed; exact source/deployment/schema/API versions; test identities by role, not passwords; the actual fixture or pilot population; executed checks and outcomes; relevant sanitised record/receipt IDs and timestamps; representative UI evidence; known exclusions; rollback/fix-forward notes; and the next measurable follow-up. Explicitly label not-run checks. Do not move missing tests into “passed with advice” when they are required by the slice.

Technical tolerances, budgets and freshness deadlines are implementation inputs to be pinned before testing, not numbers inferred after seeing results. Keep this specification aligned with the implemented method; preserve versioned historical acceptance/evidence when semantics change.
