# Functional Roadmap

**Last reconciled:** 25 August 2026

The original platform delivery roadmap is complete. Historical task status is retained in the [canonical project plan](project-plan.md) and [independent audit records](project-audits/); it is not repeated here.

## Operational baseline

The current platform provides:

- scheduled market-data ingestion and monitoring;
- independent Technical and AI Market branches with persisted Market Convergence;
- independent long-term Opportunity Assessment with events, exposures and Research & Evidence;
- approved external-opinion ingestion with provenance and consensus lineage;
- permanent-user watchlists;
- private alerts, evaluator state and event history;
- a private strategy laboratory with immutable test evidence and persisted review decisions;
- responsive public dashboards and authenticated owner workspaces;
- documented recovery, security and development workflows.

## Current decision boundary

The first strategy platform test is complete, but the first strategy is not approved for live execution. Its persisted decision is `VALIDATE_ROBUSTNESS / continue_testing` because the out-of-sample return is negative. Platform completion must not be interpreted as strategy profitability.

## Candidate future work

Future work requires explicit authorisation as a new project-plan item. The most useful next programme would be strategy validation and paper trading, including:

1. transaction costs and slippage;
2. walk-forward and additional holdout testing;
3. market-regime sensitivity;
4. parameter robustness and overfitting checks;
5. paper-trading execution, reconciliation and safety controls;
6. a new independent review gate before any live-enablement decision.

Other feature ideas should be prioritised only after observing the completed platform in normal operation. The Builder and Auditor must not invent successor work from this roadmap.
