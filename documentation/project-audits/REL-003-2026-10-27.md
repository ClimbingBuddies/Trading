# REL-003 — saved assessment dates and Convergence lineage

**Date:** 27 October 2026, Australia/Perth  
**Branch:** `reliability/restore-decision-pipeline`  
**Status:** Verified locally; not deployed. Full calendar-aware operational freshness and backend recovery remain incomplete.

## Problem and change

The live review found October AI assessments shown beside August Technical/Convergence snapshots. Although dates were displayed, the page's “Current rating,” “Highest AI Conviction” and “Latest Signal Comparison” wording could imply a current combined view.

`app/assessments/page.tsx` now describes the page as saved research and shows:

- The age of each AI assessment and saved Convergence record in **New York calendar days**, the date boundary used by Market Assessment. This is descriptive age, not a complete exchange-calendar freshness decision.
- An exact source relationship: matching displayed AI, historical/different AI, absent, or unverified. Same dates are insufficient: instrument identity and the actual `ai_assessment_id` must match. Missing, malformed or future dates do not establish a verified match.
- A count of Convergence records matching the displayed AI input, separate from other saved history. A source match is explicitly not a claim of current trading validity.
- Saved-rating and saved-confidence labels, with a warning that model confidence is not measured prediction accuracy. Historical scores and research links remain available; no historical record is removed or recalculated.

The age/lineage calculation lives in `lib/assessment-snapshot-status.mjs` with a typed declaration. It does not change source selection, AI methodology, publication eligibility, price calculations or backend state.
